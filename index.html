Hi, I'm Jonathan. I have been creating electronics projects and modifying devices since I was a young teenager. This pushed me to study Electronic Engineering at the University of Exeter where I did my integrated masters degree.

This site includes personal projects, university projects and also projects from my job at said university. My role was creating equipment and systems for data collection and automation.

This work includes experimental physics, embedded systems, pneumatics, art, analogue amplifiers, RF synthesis and modulation, manufacturing, control systems, robotics and more.
